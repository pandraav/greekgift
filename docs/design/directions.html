Direction one

Informator

The printed analysis annual. Figurine notation, a ruled page, and the two colours a chess book uses to argue: book red for the losing move, forest green for the refutation. Petrona sets everything, including the coach, so the machine sounds like an editor rather than a chatbot. Numbers use old-style figures in metadata and lining figures for scores.

Direction two

Scoresheet

The paper form you fill in at a tournament: a ruled two-column move grid, pen blue for what went right, correction magenta for what did not. Bricolage Grotesque gives the headings a slightly irregular, drawn quality; Archivo keeps the form itself neutral. The structure is the scoresheet, so the grid lines carry meaning instead of decorating.

Direction three

Night broadcast

The stream overlay: dark room, lit board, evaluation as the loudest element. Anton compresses the accuracy numbers into scoreboard slabs; Public Sans keeps the reading text calm underneath. The ten move classes get a full colour scale here, which a dark ground supports and a paper ground fights.
